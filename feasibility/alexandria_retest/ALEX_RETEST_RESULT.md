## 5. Interpretation

1. **The decision.**
   - The original association survives a stricter test: a conditional null on the radar-pixel field, a held-out likelihood statistic over 11 fold assignments, and a flexible-baseline check.
   - Under the plan's reading, the NYC and Austin non-replications are not explained by a lenient test in Alexandria.
2. **Where it holds beside the decision** (descriptive):
   - with all 341 core cells, under location uncertainty and with the full period (P2);
   - in each line of sight, under the unconditional pixel null and with a flexible baseline;
   - when radar coverage is added. Coverage alone carries nothing, so the signal is not a coherence-loss artefact.
   - It weakens at 200 m (p 0.060) and with a random forest (p 0.066).
3. **Two results limit what it means:**
   - **Smooth location (secondary 2):** with a smooth function of location in the baseline, T falls to +3.73 (p 0.253). Planted cell 4 and §2 named this as the alternative the primary test does not exclude: a smooth spatial pattern that both velocity and collapse follow. Plan §11 says a robust result is read together with this secondary.
   - **Timing (secondary 9):** the association is strongest in cells whose collapses all came before most of P1's data (T +6.51, p 0.001). It is weaker for 2016 collapses (T +3.31, p 0.140).
     - The plan names this as the pattern of a post-collapse radar artefact.
     - The 2016 group is smaller (12 positive cells against 20), so the contrast is not decisive.
   - **Persistence (secondary 11):** the velocity pattern persists into 2019–2023 (Spearman 0.90). This fits a stable spatial feature of the collapse areas. It does not separate ground motion from rebuilding.
4. **For the thesis:** Alexandria's association is real by this stricter test, but it is concurrent and site-specific. It also has the timing pattern a post-collapse artefact would show, and it weakens when location enters flexibly. It does not show that InSAR velocity predicts building damage, and it fits the NYC and Austin results only as a local, concurrent signal.

## 6. Limits (plan §11 applies in full)

- The result had been seen, and the statistic and null were chosen knowing it.
- There are 32 collapse cells in 16 blocks. The block-bootstrap interval of T includes 0.
- Labels come from news reports. The velocity window overlaps the collapses.
- The secondaries are descriptive and many; their p-values are nominal.
