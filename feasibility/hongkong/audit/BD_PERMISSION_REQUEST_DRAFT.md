**To:** Chief Officer / Technical Services, Buildings Department (enquiry@bd.gov.hk)
**Subject:** Request for permission: use of Orders Search records in non-commercial academic research

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am a [degree / position] at [university / department], working under the supervision of [supervisor, title]. Our non-commercial research project studies whether satellite radar measurements of ground and building movement (Sentinel-1 InSAR) carry information about later building-safety orders in coastal cities.

For Hong Kong we use the open datasets of buildings with outstanding Section 26 and Section 26A orders (DATA.GOV.HK). These give cumulative counts per building but no issue dates. The issue dates are shown in the Department's Orders Search.

So far we have looked up 45 buildings at a low rate (one query every few seconds) to check that the dates can be found. We kept the results on one computer and have not shared them.

I would like to ask for the following:

1. **Research use.** Confirmation that we may look up, through the Orders Search, the roughly 1,400 buildings listed in the two open datasets. The lookups would run at a low rate (one query every 3 seconds or slower). We would record each order's number, type, issue date, compliance or cancellation date, and whether it concerns common parts. We would not record the "issued to" parties. The records would be used only for this non-commercial academic research, by the research team, within our university.
2. **Publication of results.** Permission to publish, in a thesis and in peer-reviewed articles, results derived from these records: aggregate counts, statistical and model results, and maps at the level of districts or grid cells. No order records would be reproduced.
3. **Building-level derived data (optional).** Permission to deposit, with the publications, a building-level file containing only the building identifier (the open data's block ID) and the month of the first Section 26 / 26A order issued since May 2023, so that the analysis can be checked. If you prefer, we would deposit only data aggregated to grid cells.
4. **An alternative source.** Whether the Department could provide the issue dates directly, or the monthly versions of the two open datasets since May 2023. This would avoid repeated queries to the Orders Search.

We would acknowledge the Buildings Department as the source in all outputs and follow any conditions you set. We would also delete the records if you ask us to.
