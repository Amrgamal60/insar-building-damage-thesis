**Subject:** Public Information Act request: certified appraisal roll exports and parcel layers, tax years 2016–2025

To the Travis Central Appraisal District, Records Management:

Under the Texas Public Information Act (Texas Government Code, Chapter 552), I request copies of the following records:

1. **Certified appraisal roll exports for tax years 2016 through 2025.**
   - One export per year, as of certification (supplement 0, or the earliest certified supplement for that year).
   - The standard appraisal export format TCAD used that year (for example the True Prodigy or PACS "Legacy 8.0" fixed-width appraisal export now published for 2026).
   - At least these tables: PROP, IMP_INFO, IMP_DET, IMP_ATR, LAND_DET and STATE_CD, with the export layout document for each year.
2. **The same export for the most recent certified year before 2016** (for example 2015), if it is held.
3. **The parcel boundary (GIS) layer for each of those tax years**, or the nearest vintage held, with the property ID and geographic ID of each parcel.

**Format:** electronic copies, by download link or FTP, are preferred. The files as TCAD normally produces them are fine. I do not need any new compilation, filtering or redaction.

**Costs:** if charges are expected to exceed $40, please send an itemized estimate before preparing the records, as Section 552.2615 provides.

**Purpose (for information only):** academic research for a master's thesis on building foundation repairs and ground movement in Austin. Results are reported only as aggregate statistics. No owner information is published.
