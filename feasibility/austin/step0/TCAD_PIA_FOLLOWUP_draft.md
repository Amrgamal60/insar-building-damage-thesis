**Notes for the user (not part of the message):**
- **Deadlines:** under Section 552.221, TCAD must produce the records promptly. If it cannot within 10 business days, it must certify in writing a date when they will be available. For the request of 30 October, that is around 13 November (16 November if TCAD closes for Veterans Day).
- **Estimates:** if TCAD sends a cost estimate, the request is treated as withdrawn unless you answer within 10 business days (Section 552.2615). Whether to pay is your decision.
- **Why 2018 is enough for the primary analysis:** the 2018 roll alone gives reference option A. The yearly rolls only add the sale analysis (secondary 14), which runs only with deed records for every year.
