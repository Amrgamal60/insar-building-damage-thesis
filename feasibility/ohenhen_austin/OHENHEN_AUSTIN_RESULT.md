- **Holm order:** V had the smaller p, so it was tested first at 0.025. It was rejected, so U was tested at 0.05.
- **T for U:** the held-out log-likelihood gain of M1 over M0, averaged over 11 fold assignments, is **-4.65**. Its 95% block-bootstrap interval is -30.1 to +28.9 (2,000 of 2,000 resamples kept). T is negative in 11 of 11 assignments, with a range of -8.28 to -0.05.
- **V's interval:** the lower 95% bound, 1.59, is also above δ = 1.5. This is stated as a fact; the plan's readings do not have a separate category for it.
- **U's interval:** the 90% interval contains both 1 (no enrichment) and δ = 1.5. So the study neither shows nor rules out added enrichment of the size δ beyond the baseline.
- **Planted power (r3; plan §10):**
  - V at IRR 1.5: 51.2% at level 0.025, 65.2% at 0.05. At IRR 2.0: 93.6% and 97.6%.
  - U "demonstrated" at IRR 1.5 beyond the baseline: 75.8%. With no flag effect: 4.8% (cell 7) and 3.6% (cell 8).

**Reading (plan §10 wording).** The map flags houses that record more foundation repairs in the four years after its data end, but not beyond what the baseline features already show. The planted check made this the expected pattern if the map adds nothing beyond the baseline (plan §15).

- V is about the unadjusted contrast that the paper claims, for recorded repair permits in the map's own domain.
- U is about information beyond age, size, soil, terrain, location and income.
- Neither result says the map's 25-year hazard is wrong.

## 6. Interpretation

1. **The published claim holds for recorded repairs in Austin.**
   - Houses in the high and very-high risk classes recorded first foundation repairs at 3.2 times the rate of other mapped houses, in the four years after the map's data end.
   - The rate rises with class from class 1 upward (secondary 1). It holds in each year (secondary 9), after excluding new-construction areas (7) and after excluding houses near cell edges (11).
   - It does not appear with remodel permits as the outcome (8: IRR 0.91). So it is not a general difference in permit activity.
2. **The flag adds no demonstrated information beyond the baseline.**
   - Adjusting for Baseline 1 + x/y takes the IRR from 3.23 to 1.16. Within 2 km blocks it is 0.79 (secondary 6). Age alone explains little of the association (3.02).
   - The held-out gain T is negative (-4.65).
   - This is the pattern the planted check predicted if the map carries only what soil, location and the other baseline features already carry. The interval is wide enough that added enrichment of δ is not excluded.
3. **A lead for a later confirmatory test (secondary; not a finding of this study).**
   - The hazard layer is angular distortion alone, without the building-density part of the risk class. Its adjusted IRR was 1.74 (95% 1.11 to 2.77, nominal p 0.0081), with T +20.78.
   - VLM itself, per mm/yr of subsidence, was not associated in the expected direction (IRR 0.79).
   - This points to differential settlement, not the subsidence rate, as the candidate signal.
   - This is one of 13 descriptive secondaries, with nominal p, no multiplicity correction, no bootstrap interval for T, and the same outcomes. It needs its own frozen test on new outcomes or another city before it can be claimed.
   - One possible reason the risk class does worse is that building density, which it adds, is close to lot area, already in Baseline 1 (step-0 Spearman −0.48). This is not tested here.

## 7. Limits specific to this result (the plan's §12 limits all apply)

- **Inference:** r3 rejected in 7.3% of planted datasets with a 15 km-range hidden field (plan §9). V's p (0.002) is far below both Holm levels, and under r4 it is 0.0066. U's conclusion does not depend on the inference (p ≥ 0.23 under every candidate).
- **Depletion:** flagged houses left the cohort before 2022 at about twice the rate of the others (1.38% vs 0.64%). This biases V toward the null.
- **Outcomes already used:** the 2022–2025 outcomes were used by the Austin study with another predictor (plan §3). The map, the cohort rule and the analysis were frozen here before linkage.
- **The map's domain:** the map covers 51.6% of eligible houses, mostly younger and with less canopy. Over all eligible houses (secondary 4) the flag holds 7.8% of events.
- **Short follow-up:** 3.9 years against the hazard's 25-year horizon.
